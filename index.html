📖 1. Read & Listen (15 Mins)
Story: The Sad River

Sungai Mutiara was clean, and many fish lived happily in the water. Soon, people threw plastic rubbish into the drains. Factories also dumped toxic waste into the river. Many fish died because the water was poisoned. The villagers felt sad, but they worked together to clean the river again.

🔍 2. 5 Key Points (15 Mins)
🗑️
Drain Litter: Rubbish thrown in drains flows straight into rivers.
🏭
Factory Waste: Toxic chemicals from factories poison river water.
🐟
Aquatic Harm: Fish and water plants die in dirty, polluted water.
🚰
Unsafe Water: Polluted water carries germs and is unsafe to drink.
♻️
River Rescue: Recycling and clean-ups keep our rivers clean.
🔗 3. Compound Sentences
and (Joins similar ideas) Ali and Abu cleaned the riverbank.
because (Gives a reason) The fish died because the water was dirty.
but (Shows contrast) The water looks clear, but it has harmful chemicals.
